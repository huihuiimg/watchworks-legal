Watchworks is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what data the game and its third-party advertising partners may collect.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores your current level and sound preference locally using Unity PlayerPrefs. This data is not sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the game.

We do not ask for an account, name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Advertising

The game may show optional rewarded video ads. Our advertising partners may collect device advertising identifier, IP address and approximate region, device model and OS version, and ad interaction events. This data is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and - where you consent - personalize advertising.

Partners:

  • Google AdMob - policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads (fallback) - unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Where required by law (for example, in the EEA or UK), the game will ask for consent before using personalized advertising. You can decline personalized ads and still play.

3. Analytics and crash data

The current build does not include an analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If one is added, this policy will be updated before release.

4. Children

Watchworks is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your choices

  • Turn sound off in the game.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in device settings.
  • Limit ad personalization in device settings.
  • Uninstall the game to remove local progress.

6. Data retention

We do not operate a server for this game, so we do not retain personal data. Advertising partners retain data under their own policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
